# Third-party assets

The navigation icons `ic_wrist`, `ic_settings`, `ic_diagnostics`, and `ic_info` are
Google Material Symbols Outlined (`watch`, `settings`, `monitor_heart`, and `info`),
converted from the official SVG assets into Android vector drawables.
